03 / BACKSTORY HOW I GOT HERE

Most AI consulting is theatre. A McKinsey deck nobody implements, a vendor pitching the same SaaS to twelve different industries, or a junior analyst running ChatGPT prompts at three thousand dollars a day. Australian mid-market operators see through it instantly, and then wonder why nothing changes.

Vermilion Intelligence exists because I got tired of watching real businesses lose ground to that pattern. Operators who run twenty to two hundred staff, who have actual operational pain, and who want a partner who will sit at their desk and map the workflow before quoting a fee.

Most AI initiatives fail because the executive cannot tell which workflow to automate. The technology is the easy part. From The Intelligence Brief, edition 04

The model is straightforward. Fourteen-day Phase 0 discovery for ten thousand dollars, where we audit one division and surface the three workflows worth automating. If the audit lands, we build in eight to twelve weeks for forty to sixty thousand. If it does not, the discovery still pays for itself in clarity. Either way you walk away with a board-ready plan.

I write The Intelligence Brief on LinkedIn every two weeks because most decision-makers do not need another AI newsletter. They need a sharper way to ask the right questions of their own teams. Less hype, fewer demos, more skepticism applied where skepticism actually changes the call.

The best AI deployments give your best people their job back. Not save them time. Give them their job back. Sameer Chib · Workshop note, March 2026

Right now I am building The Workflow Audit, a course teaching the same discovery framework I run for clients. Six modules. Built for executives who do not want to write a line of code, but do want to know exactly which workflows in their business can become AI-native and which cannot. Cohort one launching later this year.

I am twenty-one, in Sydney, working in public, and I think the next decade of Australian business gets rebuilt by people my age who refuse to accept that the only way to deploy AI is to hire a consulting firm. If you are an operator with real workflows, scroll down and book a call. Fifteen minutes, no slides. And if you are twenty-something with a real ambition, message me anyway. The only thing I respect more than a built business is someone trying to build one.
